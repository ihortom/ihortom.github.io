Templatr User Guide

HOME / Templatr

Templatr keeps the text you type again and again, like replies, commands and snippets, and copies any of it to your clipboard with one click.

Install

  1. Download the DMG for your Mac:
    • …_arm64.dmg: Apple silicon Macs (M1 and newer)
    • …_x64.dmg: Intel Macs
  2. Open the DMG and drag Templatr into Applications.
  3. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.

Add your templates

  1. From the app menu, choose Edit Templates. Your templates file opens in your default text editor.

  2. Add templates in groups. Each group has a name followed by a list of templates:

    Greetings:
    - Hi _VAR_, thanks for reaching out! # Friendly opener
    - Good morning _VAR_,
Sign-offs:
    - Best regards
    - "Thanks again,

      The Support Team" # Multi-line template
    • Anything after # is a comment. It appears under the template as a hint and is never copied.
    • _VAR_ is a placeholder you can fill in from the app (see below).
    • For a template that spans several lines, put it in quotes and leave an empty line wherever you want a line break.
  3. Save the file, then choose Relaunch App from the menu to load your changes.

Copy a template

Click a template, and it's on your clipboard. It gets a green outline so you can see which one you copied last. Paste it wherever you need it.

Find a template

  • Search box: type any word to show only the templates that contain it.
  • Checkbox next to the search box: when ticked, the search also looks in comments, so a template can be found by the words in its hint.
  • Pick template type: show only one group. Choose All types to show every group again, or Reset to also clear the search.
  • ✕ (red button): clears the search.

Fill in placeholders (_VAR_)

Type a value, such as a name, into the Substitute value box. Every _VAR_ on screen is replaced with it and highlighted, and that's what gets copied when you click.

  • Paste: puts whatever is on your clipboard into the box.
  • ✕: clears the box, and the templates show _VAR_ again.

Save values you use often (optional)

From the menu, choose Edit Substitutes List and add one value per line: 

- Alice
- Bob
- ACME Corp

Save, then Relaunch App. When you type in the Substitute value box, matching values appear in a list below it. Use ↑ / ↓ to pick one and Enter to choose it, or click it. You can still type any value that isn't on the list.

Light and dark mode

Click the sun / moon button in the top-right corner to switch themes. Templatr remembers your choice.

Where your files are kept

Templates, saved values and settings are in a hidden .templatr folder in your home folder. Back up that folder to keep your templates, or copy it to another Mac.
